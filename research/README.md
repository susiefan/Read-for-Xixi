# Read-for-Xixi research index

This folder contains the evidence base used to frame and prototype Read-for-Xixi.

1. `01-executive-synthesis.md` — central findings and product implications.
2. `02-shared-reading-and-language.md` — dialogic reading, responsive interaction, dosage, and screen considerations.
3. `03-sign-language-and-chinese-signing.md` — archived sign-language research and product-boundary decisions.
4. `04-parent-barriers-and-expert-cost.md` — caregiver fatigue, time, confidence, access, and cost.
5. `05-avatar-ai-feasibility-and-safety.md` — avatar feasibility, limitations, and safety boundaries.
6. `06-landscape-and-opportunity.md` — relevant products, precedents, and market gap.
7. `07-parent-voice-and-toddler-response.md` — familiar-voice evidence and product implications.
8. `08-3d-cat-asset-review.md` — avatar asset evaluation and the move toward Live 2D.
9. `09-natural-voice-and-parent-voice-cloning.md` — voice synthesis, recording, consent, and implementation.
10. `10-adaptive-child-response-system.md` — evidence-informed adaptation model.
11. `sources.md` — source ledger.

Raw transcripts, book-specific content, interaction scripts, and user-session records are intentionally excluded.
