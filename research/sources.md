# Source ledger

| Source | Type | What it supports | Confidence / limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| [AAP 2024 literacy policy](https://publications.aap.org/pediatrics/article/154/6/e2024069090/199467/Literacy-Promotion-An-Essential-Component-of) | Professional policy | Start early; joyful, interactive shared reading; daily routines | High for guidance; does not prescribe one hour |
| [AAP 2024 technical report](https://publications.aap.org/pediatrics/article/154/6/e2024069091/199468/Literacy-Promotion-An-Essential-Component-of) | Technical evidence review | Mechanisms and pediatric literacy-promotion evidence | High |
| [AAP 18–23 month guidance](https://www.healthychildren.org/english/ages-stages/toddler/pages/how-to-share-books-with-your-18-to-23-month-old.aspx) | Parent guidance | Practical 10–15 minute bedtime example | Moderate; older page, still useful as concrete guidance |
| [Dowdall et al. 2020](https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1246036) | Meta-analysis, 19 RCTs | Book-sharing interventions and parent competence | High; ages 1–6 and heterogeneous programs |
| [Noble et al. 2019](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1747938X18305116) | Meta-analysis | Effects are small and sensitive to control design | High |
| [Flack et al. 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29595311/) | Meta-analysis, 38 studies | Word learning; dialogic style matters | High |
| [Madigan et al. 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32202633/) | Meta-analysis, 42 studies | Screen quantity/context and language associations | High for association; limited causality |
| [2024 screen-context review](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39102255/) | Systematic review/meta-analysis | Co-use, background TV, caregiver screen context | High for association; observational base |
| [Parent coaching RCT](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32015127/) | Randomized trial | Parentese and conversational turns | Moderate-high; modest sample |
| [30-month follow-up](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36999222/) | Longitudinal RCT follow-up | Durable language differences and mediation by turns | Moderate-high; follow-up sample 59 |
| [Hutton et al. 2015](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/25825501/) | Exploratory fMRI study | Home reading exposure and activation supporting semantic processing and mental imagery during story listening | Moderate; small correlational sample, not causal evidence |
| [Responsive parenting meta-analysis](https://publications.aap.org/pediatrics/article-pdf/144/4/e20183556/1078264/peds_20183556.pdf) | Meta-analysis | Responsive parenting associated with language | High for association; observational studies |
| [Shared-reading barriers meta-synthesis](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12326312/) | Systematic qualitative meta-synthesis | Time, fatigue, confidence, access, enjoyment barriers | Moderate-high; only nine included studies |
| [Baby-sign systematic review](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/0142723714562864) | Systematic review | No robust lasting language benefit; no detected harm | High relative to sparse evidence; search ended 2013 |
| [Kirk et al. 2013](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/j.1467-8624.2012.01874.x) | Randomized trial | No language acceleration from sign training | Moderate; only 40 infants, endpoint 20 months |
| [ASHA AAC portal](https://www.asha.org/practice-portal/professional-issues/augmentative-and-alternative-communication/) | Clinical professional guidance | AAC/signs need not suppress speech in children with communication needs | High for clinical population; not direct evidence for typical hearing toddlers |
| [CSL lexical variation](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0024384120301522) | Corpus study | North/south CSL dialect groups and lexical variation | Moderate-high |
| [China Common Sign Language release](https://en.moe.gov.cn/news/press_releases/201805/t20180524_336996.html) | Government source | Voluntary national common-sign vocabulary | High for policy history |
| [CSL general service list](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40155053/) | Corpus research | Frequency-based core CSL lexicon | Moderate-high |
| [Sign production survey](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0957417423033481) | Technical survey | Dataset and generation constraints | Moderate-high |
| [Avatar rapid review](https://www.mdpi.com/2414-4088/9/8/82) | Rapid review | Manual/nonmanual fidelity and comprehension | Moderate; rapid rather than exhaustive review |
| [Deaf-led AI review](https://aclanthology.org/2024.signlang-1.6.pdf) | Systematic review, 101 papers | Governance, representation, linguistic and dataset bias | High relevance to development ethics |
| [VL2 storybook study](https://vl2.gallaudet.edu/center-papers/607) | Primary study | Signed bilingual storybook engagement | Moderate; Deaf children and ASL/English, not target population |
| [YC The Primer](https://www.ycombinator.com/rfs#the-primer) | Vision / product inspiration | Longitudinal adaptation and tutoring ambition | Not empirical evidence |
| [BLS interpreters/translators](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/media-and-communication/interpreters-and-translators.htm) | U.S. labor statistics | Compensation benchmark | High but role is broader than sign-language teaching |
| [BLS speech-language pathologists](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/healthcare/speech-language-pathologists.htm) | U.S. labor statistics | SLP compensation benchmark | High but different role from an ASL educator |
